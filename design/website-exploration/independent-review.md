## Delivery judgment

The set is ready for owner comparison. I found no blocker that requires regenerating a design before showing these directions. All six have a conspicuous Open App entrance, a substantive play explanation and genuine documentation layouts. The guides have search, grouped navigation, article context, useful prose and an app exit. No generated rooster or substitute mascot is present. No fabricated player counts, token-price charts, mainnet availability, bridge, guaranteed rewards, NPC trader, direct chat or trustless-inference claim was found.

The six are meaningfully different identities, rather than palette swaps. The closest pair is 01/06: both use a cream ground and rounded heavy type, and both have text beside a domestic scene. Set 06 earns its separate direction through annotated ownership/collected-history plates; it is less compositionally radical than 03/04/05. Sets 02/05 both use serif display type but differ clearly in printed manual versus centered night-stage composition.

## Corrections to carry visibly into production notes

- **Supply imagery:** 01's game page places a watering can in the three-item packing box. 03's game and guide put a watering can beside the packing explanation. Water is an actual supply, represented elsewhere by a capped bottle; there is no watering-can inventory item. Replace the can with the authentic water asset in production packing diagrams. A can can remain ordinary environmental decoration where it is not presented as something to pack. This is more concrete than a tiny lettering defect, but does not invalidate the choice of style.
- **Plant vocabulary:** 02's home/game plates, 03's home/game garden and 06's home contain cabbage/tomato/vegetable-like clusters. The real catalog contains four flowers, four fruits and a wood tree. These are exploratory environmental pictures, not real saved days. Do not silently ship them as catalog examples. Use approved existing art/captures or consciously map the beds to supported plants. Set 01 already made this correction; set 04's mostly empty beds avoid it.
- **Tools wording:** 02 home says “supplies, tools, or seeds.” Its own notes acknowledge this should become “up to three different supplies.” Do not imply a new separate tool-packing mechanic.
- **Future-life wording:** Prefer explicit preparation-and-playback wording when replacing vague “check back to see what it did” / “check in” lines. The no-aging reassurance is present, but the final live copy should make it clear that a player prepares a day and then watches the saved result; visiting later does not simulate days unattended.
- **Illustration authenticity:** The new room maps, roofs, furniture, plants, eggs and gifts are concept art, often more detailed/oblique than the current renderer. Keep the gallery-level illustrative caveat visible. A chosen website must use the exact canonical rooster generator for any rooster and reconcile all product depictions with the actual top-down game. Never use an imagegen bird to fill the deliberate absence.

## Followups after the owner chooses

1. Convert the selected design into semantic live HTML/CSS, with selectable text, keyboard navigation, visible focus, real links/search and verified contrast. Images are design guidance, not deliverable web UI.
2. Check 360px and 390px screens with real content and ordinary document scrolling. Keep body text at least 16px, sensible line lengths and touch targets. Collapse guide sidebars to accessible contents controls; do not shrink a desktop guide to fit a phone.
3. Preserve Open App visibility/reachability, returning-owner context, and exact-life/world/collection/token/recovery URLs before changing the public/app split. Existing direct links must keep their destination.
4. Derive wallet/world/contract/price facts from one current source. The two worlds remain testnets with separate lives, belongings and NHC; NEAR uses Meteor, Robinhood supports Rabby/EVM. More detailed payment or trust articles need current exact facts rather than inferred marketing copy.
5. Keep website/docs content immediately useful without loading the game runtime or waiting for ambient loops. Respect reduced motion. Do not animate growing inventory/beds in a way that suggests offline progression.
6. Use current authentic captures or existing environmental assets for game inserts. Preserve the seeded canonical rooster shape, palette, accessories and poses unchanged.

No recommendation here selects a winner for the owner. Small generative lettering and literal image spacing are not release blockers for an exploratory gallery; they must be corrected and tested when a real site is implemented.
