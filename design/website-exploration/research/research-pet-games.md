# Official pet / cozy / pixel game website research

Research date: 4 November 2026 (Asia/Bangkok). Researcher: pet/game-site agent. This is a reference pack for six website mockup directions, not a proposal to change the game or publish anything.

## Evidence boundary

I opened current official pages with the web text tool, followed their navigation, and inspected the exposed page hierarchy, text, image descriptions and destination links. I did not render their pixels or benchmark their animation, JavaScript weight, accessibility or loading speed. Visual observations requiring screenshots belong to the PM's Codex Browser inspection. Animation/performance recommendations below are our design inferences, not measured claims about these sites. Existing player counts, release banners and chain claims below are those sites' marketing copy, not facts to imitate or independently validated metrics.

## 1. Neko Atsume — tiny navigation, observation as the proposition

Official homepage: https://www.nekoatsume.com/en/

Official play guide: https://www.nekoatsume.com/en/about.html

Official support: https://www.nekoatsume.com/en/support.html

The homepage exposes only the game, How to Play, Support and download destinations. The guide explicitly treats watching as enjoyable, then explains Catbook, Album, Shop and Remodel with screenshots. Help topics live on a separate FAQ page. This is unusually close to NEARlyHuman's appeal: the pleasure of watching little autonomous lives, with a handful of supporting controls.

Transferable inference: put the emotional reason to watch before the mechanics. A website could use a small house scene and a line like “A little life. A mind of its own.” The play guide should show the three-part rhythm with actual game imagery, not become a list of every AI action. Keep the docs reading layer clean and outside the stage. Do not imitate the long, undifferentiated support FAQ; give our docs short named sections and search.

## 2. Stardew Valley — the world is the sales material

Official homepage: https://www.stardewvalley.net/

Official FAQ: https://www.stardewvalley.net/faq/

Official presskit: https://www.stardewvalley.net/press/

The first copy sets a concrete situation, followed by real gameplay images and six clear activity propositions. Persistent navigation separates Blog, Forums, Wiki, Media, Merch and FAQ. The FAQ offers the basic definition/platform routes and sends technical problems to a troubleshooting guide. The presskit separately groups facts, media and creator details.

Transferable inference: use the house, garden and domestic scenes to make the genre legible immediately. Keep the homepage story short and visibly link the reference material. NEARlyHuman has a browser app rather than many storefronts, so one Open App is simpler than copying a platform grid. Do not copy the full eight-item navigation or six-feature structure by habit; our product is smaller. A presskit can exist later without adding another primary nav item now.

## 3. Tamagotchi — emotional premise and character identity before instructions

Official US site: https://tamagotchi-official.com/us/

Official Original manual: https://tamagotchi-official.com/manual/toy/original/Original_webmanual.pdf

Official Connection manual: https://tamagotchi-official.com/manual/toy/connection/connection_web_manual_IS_EN.pdf

The brand site distinguishes News, History, Characters, Series, Products, Video and shops. Its short premise introduces quirky life forms, their care needs and the everyday relationship. Product-specific manuals carry actual operation. The site has image-led character and product sections, but is fundamentally a franchise/catalogue destination rather than a playable browser-game funnel.

Transferable inference: explain that this is a relationship with a specific rooster, not an AI dashboard. A very small “You prepare. They decide.” message can carry much of the idea. Keep documentation practical and separate. Do not borrow catalogue density, promotional carousels or fabricated character redesigns; NEARlyHuman's existing procedural rooster artwork is canonical and must stay intact.

## 4. Usagi Shima — a small, personal game's restrained information structure

Official site: https://usagishima.net/

Official original game introduction: https://usagishima.net/182-2/

Official About: https://usagishima.net/about/

Official FAQ: https://usagishima.net/frequently-asked-questions/

The current navigation is Home, Presskit, About, Support. The game introduction describes becoming caretaker of an island, placing toys/buildings and observing visitors; it contains a trailer and platform destinations. About provides an identifiable solo creator and separate social links. Current home content also foregrounds merchandise/release news, showing how a mature project's homepage can evolve away from a concise first-game explanation.

Transferable inference: our website can feel authored and friendly with only a few routes. “About” need not become a corporate team wall. Show one lived-in environment rather than a busy feature collage. Keep time-sensitive news/merchandise out of the first-use path unless they serve the player's immediate next step.

## 5. Sunflower Land — strongest direct marketing / app / docs separation

Official marketing: https://sunflower-land.com/

Official app destination: https://sunflower-land.com/play

Official docs: https://docs.sunflower-land.com/

Current docs index: https://docs.sunflower-land.com/llms.txt

Current start guide: https://docs.sunflower-land.com/start-here/how-to-start

The marketing page opens with a short farming/trading proposition, one play destination, low-friction entry assurances and a game-map image. Later sections show activities, ownership, updates, team, community and FAQ. Docs group Start Here, Game Guides, Web3 & Economy, project information and support; detailed currencies/wallets are separated from crop/recipe guides. The current start guide prioritises Google login over a wallet path and points to an in-game tutorial.

Transferable inference: visitors should understand the game before learning the infrastructure. Keep Open App persistent, and put practical wallet/network information in an obvious docs article. Our native/RH wallet requirements differ, so do not borrow “free/no wallet required” promises, user metrics or currency claims. Docs need not mirror every category in this larger game's encyclopaedia. An “Official links” page is useful once several networks and wallets exist.

## 6. Pixels — clear app destination, but avoid competing economic CTAs

Official marketing: https://pixels.xyz/

Actual app destination: https://play.pixels.xyz/

Official help: https://help.pixels.xyz/en/

Official current litepaper: https://litepaper.pixels.xyz/

The site repeatedly leads to the same playable domain. The first section introduces an adventurous social world and also offers staking/community actions. Help is task-based: searchable Account, Gameplay, Currency, NFTs, Community categories, with Play Now in the help footer. The whitepaper is separately economic/strategic. Some current homepage feature copy is placeholder text, a reminder that a polished page must still be editorially finished.

Transferable inference: maintain the same obvious app escape route from marketing and docs. Give “Care & days” and “Wallet & ownership” distinct docs headings. Do not place token-staking/economic actions beside Open App, imitate its social/MMO promises, or reproduce its placeholder editorial state. Our game is intimate and individual, so it needs a much calmer homepage than a large community platform.

## 7. Neopets — useful as a complexity counterexample

Official portal: https://portal.neopets.com/ (403 in text fetch; no visual claim)

Official general introduction: https://www.neopets.com/help/generalinfo.phtml

Official returning-player guide: https://portal.neopets.com/news/welcome-back

The official introductory help describes creating an account/pet before the many activities. The returning-player article is explicitly a short re-entry guide addressing account recovery, persistent names, security, prizes and companion-app access. Root/portal pages rejected this tool, so their current layout and CTA placement need browser verification. I did not rely on third-party complaint threads as evidence.

Transferable inference: returning players and new players need different destinations. Our Open App should preserve a returning player's game context rather than force a repeated marketing tour. A docs “First day” article can provide a bounded path without enumerating every feature or exposing future choices. Avoid making the public website into a portal of unrelated side-products.

## 8. WEBFISHING — demonstrate verbs and atmosphere with authentic play

Developer-owned original project: https://lamedeveloper.itch.io/webfishing

Current official store page linked by that developer: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3146520/WEBFISHING/

Official webfishing.info was inaccessible to the text tool. webfishing.net was discovered but excluded: the official authorship was not established and its generic marketing copy should not become evidence.

The developer's itch page clearly distinguishes the old jam version from the current Steam release. The current store page offers a brief social/fishing premise, authentic media and concrete activity sections for collection, upgrades, friends and toys. It demonstrates what one does, rather than explaining its renderer or networking implementation.

Transferable inference: use moments from the actual day to make the game understandable, with one concise caption per moment. Small atmospheric loops are more relevant than a large cinematic intro or separate cartoon universe. Keep the official playable version obvious and avoid multiple equally prominent “start” destinations.

## Cross-site synthesis for NEARlyHuman

These are recommendations drawn from the sources, not claims they already exist in our app:

1. **One front door:** top navigation of The Game / How it Works / Docs, plus a strongly contrasting Open App button. On phones a compact menu may hold the secondary routes; Open App remains directly visible.
2. **The first-screen question:** what is this, why is it enjoyable, and where do I enter? A domestic pixel-art scene and one sentence should answer the first two; the button answers the third.
3. **No wallet before interest:** browsing marketing/docs should not need wallet connection. All wallet/network selection belongs in the existing app flow and focused docs.
4. **Three ordinary verbs:** hatch a life; pack a day; see what happens. A linked page can explain the day without displaying Jev's unrevealed answers.
5. **Show ownership as behaviour:** a small saved-life / inventory / purse illustration can explain the NFT, with chain-specific technical details one level deeper. Do not lead with a token chart, yield language, fake partner wall or invented onchain guarantees.
6. **Readability changes by layer:** expressive large display type for the introduction; ordinary highly legible body type and a proper sidebar for docs. Pixel typography can accent navigation/headings but should not turn article paragraphs into a game HUD.
7. **Motion should reward attention:** two or three tiny garden/window/mail/sky loops; immediate first paint; static readable content usable while art loads. This is a production recommendation, not measured comparative performance.
8. **Preserve returning-player intent:** Open App directly enters/resumes the existing app. Do not restart an intro cinematic, sample day or network explanation on each visit.
9. **Docs in visitor order:** Start Here (overview, first rooster, first day); Care & Home (needs, supplies, garden, workshop, events); Ownership (NFT, purse, transfer/death); Wallets & Networks; Reference (rules, contracts, official links, FAQ). Keep developer/operator material a clearly secondary group rather than mixing it into the player guide.
10. **Copy should be truthful:** live testnets are NEAR and Robinhood; no mainnet launch, new merchants, co-op play, active offline ageing, risk-free earnings, free NFT mint or no-wallet assertion should appear as an implemented promise.

## Useful design contrasts for six independent directions

The same content can be expressed as (a) a game-world window and quiet editorial columns, (b) a compact cartridge/manual system, (c) a specimen/living-life archive with warm cream paper, (d) a vividly coloured domestic pixel map and sharp modern type, (e) an evening observatory / lit windows and readable light docs, or (f) a playful small garden almanac. These are optional independent composition territories, not a requirement to use those themes and not references to copy exact protected artwork.
