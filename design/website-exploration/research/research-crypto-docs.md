# Crypto game and documentation reference research

Researched 4 November 2026. Primary sources only. This agent read current official web/text content and information architecture; it did **not** inspect rendered pixels, animation, load timings or mobile usability. Root is collecting browser screenshots separately. Visual suggestions below are design inferences, not claims about unseen pages. No app, financial service or chain changes were made.

## Useful comparables

### 1. Aavegotchi: the closest ownership metaphor

Sources: [homepage](https://www.aavegotchi.com/), [official docs](https://docs.aavegotchi.com/), [older Gotchiverse getting started](https://docs.gotchiverse.io/overview/getting-started).

The current homepage introduces an onchain companion, then organizes value into playable, customizable and tradable behavior. Play Games and View Docs are distinct exits. An FAQ answers acquisition and chain questions after the concept is introduced. Its metaphor connects a pet to assets more concretely than a contract diagram. NEARlyHuman can show a little life carrying home, possessions and purse together. Avoid copying yield/value-growth claims, ecosystem-scale navigation or old Polygon instructions: the current homepage says Base while older Gotchiverse instructions still refer to Polygon. This mismatch is a particularly relevant warning for our two separate testnet worlds.

### 2. Axie Infinity: separate the world from the practical launcher

Sources: [homepage](https://axieinfinity.com/), [current start guide](https://support.axieinfinity.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405373226779-Getting-Started-with-Axie-Infinity), [app](https://app.axieinfinity.com/).

The marketing site holds games, lore, marketplace and community. The app is a separate practical destination. Its current guide explicitly separates wallet/account/assets/download steps, with a skip when social wallet creation already made the account. This division supports NEARlyHuman's requested website plus Open App button. Borrow the clear portal boundary, not the sprawling multigame menu or mandatory setup sequence as the landing page. Our visitors can understand the life before connecting, while wallet/network/funds belong in the app and task-specific help.

### 3. Sunflower Land: gameplay first, economy second

Sources: [homepage](https://sunflower-land.com/?promo=INTRO), [official docs](https://docs.sunflower-land.com/), [complete docs index](https://docs.sunflower-land.com/llms.txt), [official repository/links](https://github.com/sunflower-land/sunflower-land).

The homepage leads with farming and a direct play button, explains ownership briefly and answers likely questions in an encyclopedia-style FAQ. It puts wallet necessity and token purpose in answers rather than making the hero a token dashboard. Docs group Start Here, game guides, then Web3/economy; cropping, weather and crafting are separate navigable subjects. Borrow a recognizable world, short verb-led loop and approachable handbook. Do not copy free/no-wallet claims: NEARlyHuman hatching and accepted days require the selected testnet wallet and testnet funds. Do not reproduce its large feature catalog or partnership/player metrics.

### 4. Loot Survivor and Provable Games: seeing a run before joining

Sources: [live game launcher](https://www.lootsurvivor.io/), [studio homepage](https://provable.games/), [official documentation](https://docs.provable.games/), [getting started](https://docs.provable.games/lootsurvivor/guide/getting-started).

The launcher exposes game modes and public Watch links; the studio site presents the game, collectibles and documentation. The docs explicitly separate Players from Developers and provide a practical start path. A public saved NEARlyHuman life is an excellent wallet-free proof of what a day feels like, with no invented earning outcomes. Borrow compact public observation and a clear player/developer divide. Avoid copying multiple modes, live statistics, wagering or claims that nobody can shut down/corrupt the experience: our Jev relay and hosted worker remain trusted services. The start-guide text and current launcher list different mode terminology, so verify linked help against the actual app.

### 5. Fren Pet: pet-first onboarding and progressive help

Sources: [game](https://www.frenpet.xyz/), [linked documentation site](https://fren-pet-docs.vercel.app/), [gameplay page](https://fren-pet-docs.vercel.app/gameplay).

The docs overview introduces the pet actions, then a short adoption/name/onboarding sequence. Gameplay, shop, rewards and contracts each have their own sidebar destination, plus page contents and next-page navigation. Funding and transaction limits are explained below the initial loop. This is a good pattern for showing a concrete next step without a front-page protocol lecture. Do not borrow its automatic wallet creation, free mode, sponsored daily transaction allowance or survival rules. The homepage web extractor returned only a loading shell, so its visual/navigation behavior needs browser inspection before making stronger claims.

### 6. Parallel / Colony: make AI agency tangible

Sources: [studio/game directory](https://parallel.life/), [Colony page](https://parallel.life/colony), [original design paper](https://paper.parallel.life/colony_paper_v1.pdf).

The current Colony page has quick facts, official app links, gameplay examples and FAQs about actual AI interactions. It explains conversational choices and generative challenges through activities rather than calling the whole experience merely AI-powered. NEARlyHuman should similarly show supplies leading to the rooster's own decisions, with sealed future choices. Borrow one large world scene and a few distinct examples; avoid copying sci-fi visuals, download CTAs, direct chat, generated equipment or the older whitepaper's features as current promises. Our game does not accept open-ended player directives and its artwork is the owner's procedural generator.

### 7. Guild of Guardians: a useful middle layer between promotion and reference

Sources: [homepage](https://www.guildofguardians.com/), [How to Play](https://www.guildofguardians.com/how-to-play), [FAQ](https://www.guildofguardians.com/faq), [new browser-game start guide](https://www.guildofguardians.com/news/guild-of-guardians-dungeons-early-access-guide).

The homepage presents world, role and activities; Learn the Game leads to a separate visual primer. How to Play groups a few major actions before deeper progression and NFT explanations. This intermediate page is valuable for NEARlyHuman: visitors can understand Hatch, Pack and Watch without opening a dense manual. Avoid copying the long hero roster, update/news-first order, download banner or combat complexity. This project now has multiple games, so ensure a named guide belongs to the specific current product.

### 8. Uniswap documentation: adjacent information-design reference

Sources: [developer docs](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs), [quick start](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/get-started/quickstart), [user start guide](https://support.uniswap.org/hc/en-us/articles/48552618548493-Where-to-start-with-Uniswap).

This is not a game comparable. It is useful for task routing: developer documentation separates integration methods and audiences, while user help explains wallet, funding and connection as practical steps with skip paths. NEARlyHuman should keep a player handbook separate from contracts/developer material, use topic search and article contents, and provide the actual next button. Do not import swap/token charts, protocol jargon or multichain portfolio claims. Our two worlds have separate assets and no bridge.

## Shared patterns worth trying in the six directions

1. A visitor sees the world and the plain game promise before wallet connection.
2. One strong **Open App** action in the header and hero. Guide is visibly secondary. Avoid two equally bright buttons.
3. A short intermediate **How it plays** page: hatch a unique NFT, pack up to three supplies, watch its own choices, return for another saved day. A small real example can illustrate this without revealing a newly prepared day's plan.
4. A focused **Handbook / Docs** entry point with two routes: Start your first day and Browse the reference. Game Guide / Ownership & NHC / Wallets & Worlds / Technical is a sensible compact hierarchy.
5. On desktop: readable article column, grouped left navigation, modest right page contents. On phones: current article plus an obvious Guide menu/search; no permanently occupying sidebar.
6. Consistent Open App exit throughout docs and a plain Back to website link; readers must not become trapped in a whitepaper.
7. A small factual testnet/world label and clear wallet guide. Keep protocol details expandable and never merge RH/NEAR funds or imply a bridge.
8. Public life links demonstrate real ownership and replay. They are proof of an actual game, not a marketing carousel of fabricated token balances.
9. Environment-led art, restrained little motion and authentic game captures. No fake returns, invented active-player numbers, audit seals or partner strips.

## NEARlyHuman facts for designers

Read against current README. The phrase is **A little life. A mind of its own.** The asset is an NFT rooster with a saved home, inventory, intentions and NHC purse. Jev makes legal choices; the player selects supplies, not a command script. A prepared day is saved before watching. Nothing ages while away. There are four productive beds and a wood workshop; finished goods may ship, sales are not guaranteed. NHC is utility with no cash-redemption promise. Robinhood Testnet and native NEAR Testnet are separate worlds; neither mainnet is enabled. The original rooster generator/artwork must remain unchanged. No NPC trader, direct chat or shared economy exists. No newly chosen action should be revealed during loading or on a landing-page preview for the owner's unwatched day.

## Suggested minimum website map

- `/`: approachable world, concise loop, ownership proof, Open App.
- `/how-it-plays`: three-step visual overview, daily care/garden/workshop examples, link into app.
- `/docs`: start guide and browse topics.
- `/docs/first-day`: wallet/world, hatch, pack, confirm, watch; practical current details.
- `/docs/life-and-care`, `/docs/garden`, `/docs/workshop`, `/docs/weather-and-visitors`.
- `/docs/ownership-and-nhc`: NFT-bound purse, withdrawals, transfers, remember/death, no redemption promise.
- `/docs/wallets-and-worlds`: supported wallets, testnet gas, separate worlds, no bridge.
- `/docs/technical`: deployed contracts, trusted relay, source and verification evidence.

This map is a proposal for later implementation, not work already deployed. Avoid inventing extra marketing pages solely to fill navigation.
