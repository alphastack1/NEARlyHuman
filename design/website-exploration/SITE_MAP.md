# Candidate website and documentation map

This is a content/flow proposal for the six mockup directions. It creates no application routes. Final structure and art will follow owner selection.

## Public front door

- **Home:** a recognisable world, the one-sentence premise and Open App. A quiet visual play explanation or link follows. No wallet connection, token purchase, forced film or compulsory onboarding here.
- **The game:** hatch a life, pack a few supplies, watch a day unfold. Show house/garden/workshop as real activities. Explain that the player helps and Jev chooses. Ownership is one life carrying its home, stock and purse, rather than a separate investment pitch.
- **Guide:** a short task-first start and searchable topic groups. Keep Open App and the route back to Home visible.

Use a wordmark/Home link, The game, Guide and one filled Open App action. A Source/Contracts footer can hold factual verification links. Add news, team, tokenomics charts, community channels or press only when there is real relevant content; they are not required slots in a crypto template.

## Three entry journeys

**New visitor:** Home → understands premise and world → Open App → existing world/name/wallet/hatch flow. Watching an older example is optional and clearly labelled; it must have a visible exit. Marketing does not repeat wallet setup.

**Returning owner:** Open App → the existing selected world/life/recovery state. No mandatory intro or example day. The website cannot clear authentication, pending operation IDs, local selection or payment recovery.

**Shared NFT visitor:** current exact-life link → that exact world/collection/token. Marketing is an optional About/Guide route; a new Home must not capture the existing NFT query and send the person to a generic example. Legacy root/query links need explicit preservation if /app is introduced later.

## Guide topics

| Group | Articles | Information purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start here | Your first day; Worlds & wallets | Concrete next actions, supported wallets, testnet funds and first hatch. |
| Life at home | Care & supplies; Garden; Workshop; Weather & visitors | Practical questions arising while watching. Four beds, wet bedtime growth, finite yields, wood recipes, real event effects. |
| What you own | Your NFT & purse; NHC; Sharing & transfers; Remembered lives | Saved life/stock/purse continuity, current-owner rights, withdrawal including after death, separate worlds, no promised cash redemption. |
| Under the hood | Jev & saving; Contracts & official links; Troubleshooting | What AI chooses, what contracts enforce, relay trust, current deployments, transaction/retry distinctions. |

A desktop article can use a grouped left index, a readable central column and a small optional page outline. A phone uses a Guide contents control, search and the article; sidebars cannot consume the reading width. Navigation follows the reader's task, not source-code packages.

## Example: Your first day

1. **Choose a world.** Native NEAR Testnet uses Meteor; Robinhood Chain Testnet uses an EVM wallet such as Rabby. The app displays the current world and payment before confirmation. Worlds have separate lives and balances.
2. **Hatch a life.** Name and hatch the NFT explicitly. The confirmation leads to its original procedurally generated rooster and saved home. No marketing Connect Wallet action is needed first.
3. **Prepare a day.** Choose up to three different supplies, with at most one seed packet/sapling. Review the selection and fee in the existing flow. A free ownership sign-in and paid onchain day acceptance are different actions; never describe all wallet requests as free.
4. **Watch what happens.** Replies leave sealed thoughts; the film becomes ready after saving. Jev's upcoming choices are not displayed. Playback can pause or finish without advancing another biological day.

“Nothing ages while you're away” is useful reassurance. Detailed prices belong beside actual payment and in current world help, not baked into decorative home art. Help should offer the next relevant action; a failed confirmed transaction is recorded, not erased or automatically refunded.

## Production constraints for later implementation

Keep the public presentation lightweight and separate from the Phaser/game runtime. Start with static content and small deliberate art loops; no need for heavy video, a CMS, a token dashboard or another auth layer. Preserve the canonical rooster generator exactly, and insert its actual art wherever the selected direction uses a portrait or actor. Mockup garden/house illustrations define atmosphere, not new playable geometry.

Contracts/wallet/network pages must derive from or be checked against current deployment/config data. Neither mainnet is enabled. Do not suggest asset bridging, NPC commerce, direct Jev chat, audited/trustless operation, guaranteed earnings or offline progression. Route protection and real phone verification belong to implementation after a visual direction is chosen.
