## Shared boundaries

All full scenes show precomputed recorded NPC actions. Pause/Skip/inspection change presentation only; they do not feed, heal, repair or steer the rooster. Different treatment/response variants may be independent fixtures rather than one chronological episode.

Inputs are whole single-use units spent by the recorded action, not inferred from tools remaining in a picture. Effects and particles are presentation; they add no extra damage, consumption, power, growth or randomness. Generic meter fills cannot define eligibility or serve as an electrical-power meter.

Living-bedtime lifetimes include creation day. Repair/movement never renews age. Failed attempts, replay and real-world absence do not age furniture, patches, plants or conditions. Underlying damage persists under temporary mitigation.

There are exactly two playable plots; three-card sheets show one target in alternative states. Compost fertilization and soil restoration are separate effects. Shelter supplies no water/growth. Medicine, bandage and soap keep their distinct targets.

Generated geometry, object-closeup scale and accessory pixels remain illustrative. Use the construction plan and actual procedural source for pivots, collision, occlusion, native scale and phone readability. A guide is not proof of completed gameplay or asset compatibility.
