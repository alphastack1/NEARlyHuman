## Shared implementation meaning

- Unknown saves only recheck existing work. Confirmed Failed/released state permits a fresh request and signature.
- Expiry availability alone does not unlock. Release is gas-only; a pending release offers no duplicate paid action. Confirmed release returns to a fresh quote without advancing biology.
- A missing replay packet keeps compact saved-outcome access. Ownership/account changes remove stale care eligibility.
- Memorials retain the NFT and final survived level; a fatal day is not another survived level. Trial completion alive and fatal third trial day use different truthful life states.
- Real-world absence changes no needs, deadlines, crop progress or output ages. Wound and Infection require different treatment; supplies do not force Jev to use them.
- Delivery is credited once in the prepared record, never by watching it again. Garden uses two plots; watering changes moisture, not instant maturity. Harvest rewards are immediate rather than a new stored supply type.
- Soap, leisure and all 15 catalog items keep their finite authored roles; props do not introduce a player-command interface or an extra economy.
