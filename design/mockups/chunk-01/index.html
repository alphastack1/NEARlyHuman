NH-005

Inspecting water without selecting it

NH-007

Saved day and recorded playback

Visual reference · copy note

Build use

Ready shows the final survived state, such as LV04 / SAVED04. Day-four playback can correctly show the earlier LV03 / DAY04 snapshot. The HUD follows the receipt being watched.

State boundary

The day is saved before playback. Watching, restarting, and skipping require no inference, signature, payment, or second application of mechanics. No live feed/heal/rescue controls appear.

Known hierarchy note

“Watch the recorded day” is supporting text, not another button. Start day is primary; View saved outcome is the quiet alternate. Pause and Skip to summary stay inside the square.

NH-009

One stove, three fixture states

Visual reference · reviewed

Build use

Swap visible state on one fixture without changing its footprint, approach tile, or camera scale. A temporary patch is visually distinct from toolkit repair. These are object close-ups, not three additional full game pages.

State boundary

The patch lasts two living bedtimes, creation day included. It retains underlying damage and does not age through absence, Failed attempts, or replay. Exact eligibility/effects come from the master rules.

Review note

Three states of one stove: intact, damaged, temporary wood patch. Same anchor/identity, underlying damage retained; two living bedtimes including creation day. Object reference only, not completed action/expiry sequence.
