The world extends beyond view. Keep a stable apparent scale, active target/feet/held prop and adjoining route visible; no whole-house zoom target, room tabs or loading between rooms. Camera leads a doorway gently and clamps at world bounds. Foreground walls/lintels fade or cut away instead of hiding contact. Full visual bounds include actual tall headgear around the fixed feet pivot.

Inspection pins the replay receipt, message, values and camera. Close leaves replay paused until explicit Resume. Preparation detail pins a completed choice while the real job continues. Reduced motion uses stable cuts or minimal easing with the same route/receipt meaning.

Travel is presentation within the recorded slot: no per-tile energy, extra consumption, accident rolls or Jev calls. Route fallback reaches the safe endpoint once without replaying effects. Exact path collision, native accessory bounds and phone playfield protection remain implementation checks.
