The two-life stale-job check used actual local runtime verification/authentication but **synthetic secondary read/wake responses**, with expiry stopped at gas estimation; it is a targeting test, not evidence of a second accepted request or expiry settlement.

The retained-payment-marker checks likewise used explicit synthetic accepted-state DTOs. They distinguish an accepted day acknowledgement from an unresolved wallet payment; they do not mint, withdraw or expire real funds. The actual contract withdrawal/expiry behavior remains covered by the separate local chain proof. Their additional UI preflight rejection checks that recovery controls remain usable when a wallet estimate fails.
