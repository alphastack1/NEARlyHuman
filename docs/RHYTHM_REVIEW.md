# A quieter day with a better rhythm — 2 November 2026

This pass began with three independent reviews of the actual public game: day pacing, routine variety, and physical animation. The live baseline was `index-CPacLGh4.js`, using the canonical NORI #4 day-three recording. The [before/after gallery](rhythm-review/index.html) separates natural full-day evidence from explicitly seek-based detail checks.

## What the live reviews found

The day had a coherent physical route, but its middle gave too much weight to staying in one chair. The natural live watch took **69.646 seconds**. The seated stretch lasted roughly **14.3 seconds**, repeating relaxation, the same sitting result, generic quiet text, another relaxation and a mundane sitting choice. Main work began at about 45.9 seconds.

Those repetitions were genuine saved Jev choices: relaxation at slots 21 and 31, then mundane sitting at 41. The model had alternatives. Replacing them with invented errands would misrepresent the recording. The problem was the repeated presentation of a continuous rest as separate fresh arrivals and identical empty-time captions.

The existing cameo planner also had two demonstrated gaps. NORI's ordinary anchors did not include its only eligible window/garden-view locations, so no cameo was selected. In other days it could choose the last record of a condensed quiet group, which the renderer never visits. A labelled synthetic witness reproduced the latter in 16 of 32 samples; it was not a new biological day or model result.

## Refinements

- A continuing rest at the same seat gets a shorter work beat: 1.1 seconds for relaxation and 0.85 seconds for genuine mundane sitting. First relaxation stays 2.9 seconds, a nap 3.5 seconds and main work 10.5 seconds. Walking retains its 115 world-pixels/second gait.
- Strictly unchanged quiet spans receive 1–1.35 seconds of work instead of the old fixed 1.85-second minimum. This does not merge real choices, events or state changes. Every original receipt remains inspectable.
- Quiet captions refer to the actual place, phase and furniture contact. They stay stable during that context rather than restarting at every internal record. Successful, event-free continued relaxation keeps an ongoing-rest caption across its impact; recorded log/choice text remains unchanged. Failure, death and event feedback retain their existing paths.
- A continuous contact clock lets a seeded small gesture settle naturally into ordinary idle behavior. It survives quiet records and phase boundaries and resets when the rooster actually travels or the player seeks. A zero-distance seat transition no longer briefly draws walking feet.
- Cameos are selected only at records playback actually renders. A calm quiet moment beside a recorded garden plot can now host one harmless garden cue. Garden effects draw above the plot; window effects keep their separate depth. This creates no new errand, reward, inventory change or model choice.

An actual local detail check through repeated sitting and departure improved from 11.837 to 8.393 seconds after the matched player seek. The caption stayed stable through the continuing relaxation, and the genuine mundane choice retained its recorded intent/result. Another targeted motion sequence through bedtime improved from 40.652 to 36.130 seconds. These two checks are seek-based presentation observations, separate from the full natural-day measurement.
