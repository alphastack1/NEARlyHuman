## Ranked critique

1. **The opening commute dominates every day.** The source-derived examples estimate a 21–25 second mailbox/pantry round trip. The actual MISO capture confirms the same pattern. Keep the continuous door/path/return journey, but use modest route-aware travel cadence and clearer carrying/put-away stages. Do not globally speed up the main project or remove all calm moments.
2. **Real surprises are easy to miss.** The actual MISO Day28 wing mishap happens in the receipt presented as `Feet up.`; SUNNY's dawn fixture break happens under the generic first quiet action. A concise event-aware headline/note and a brief reaction using existing canonical poses can make the real event land. Do not invent a chair accident or an injury where only a fixture broke.
3. **Some captions misdescribe the moment.** SUNNY's later Breakfast still says `Breakfast first.`. Empty interlude receipts have generic quiet titles in source, but the actual Player filters those and retains the preceding action caption; it does **not** display `Just a moment.` seven times. The observed issue is the sleepy first beat and stale action continuity, not seven visible quiet labels.
4. **The substantial project needs the clearest contact and payoff.** Preserve a little time to plant, cook or harvest; distribute multi-stop garden work naturally rather than making early contacts token pauses and leaving all work at the final bed. Show actual item/fixture changes at their meaningful contact. The apple being held is useful, readable feedback and does not need an unrelated celebratory overlay.
5. **The ending loses the day's identity.** `A little day, well lived.` plus duplicated Day/Level tells little about why that particular day mattered or what to consider next. A successful main-result headline and at most one actual final-state care/growth hint give tomorrow a purpose, with Prepare tomorrow remaining the sole main action.

## Ending design review

Derive the headline from the actual successful main receipt, not merely the chosen plan label. A failed/unavailable plan should not be reported as completed. `QuietMain` can produce a restful-day line. Earned NHC should appear only when the recorded reward is positive.

For the one forward hint, prefer an unresolved welfare condition, using actual severity/deadline data. Then prefer a ready garden over remaining growth, and only then an existing wood amount. Avoid saying the rooster plans to build a boat unless such an intention was actually saved. Do not use fake Jev quotes.
