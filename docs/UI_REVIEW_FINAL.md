# Player UI refinement handoff

The App/menu/style source is frozen. The PM approved the cream surround and the wide, short-screen layout after reviewing actual frames beside VR01. No known P1 remains in the reviewed v1 flows. The PM owns staging, the single production deployment and the live smoke test.

The complete disposition is in [UI_COVERAGE.md](UI_COVERAGE.md): 203 named UI rows/variants, not 203 independent tests. Of those rows, 72 have direct project/isolated/scenario UI evidence, nine reference renderer/movie evidence, three are explicit viewport/text surrogates, 108 are shared layout/logic derivatives, and eleven are deferred. Individual notes explain partial or unexecuted variations rather than treating every label as a separate passing test.

## Result

- Cold trial, summary and memorial contexts retain a watch entry. Saved Days reads bounded metadata; older movies and literal saved actions do not replace current NFT facts, acknowledge an unplayed latest day or offer a historical next-day action.
- Old async ownership, quote, acceptance and replay responses cannot steal a newer context. Actual valid newer-base quotes and ownership errors refresh the canonical view before any send. Read errors belong to their navigation/read scope and clear on a successful retry or a confirmed new context.
- DOM menus cannot trigger Phaser object inspection underneath. UI and renderer input gates plus native canvas-origin checks close the exact chair→pause→status→options→help pointer failure; direct canvas inspection still works after closing menus.
- Delivery remains zero to three distinct types. Empty slots open the direct fifteen-item picker; item choice adds once, Back does not mutate, filled slots remove, and a fourth type requires an explicit removal. All fifteen Info readers preserved selection and restored focus.
- Saved status exposes exact needs, every condition/deadline, stock, two plots and relevant house/output lifetimes. Full stock counts fit at 320px; mature plots explicitly say Ready and show their growth maximum. Replay item details show recorded stock, without a future care kit.
- Reading cards use the visible viewport and its offsets, with one body scroller and a reachable footer. Ordinary supply/pager targets are at least 44 pixels high. The compact HUD uses reviewed 44×32 targets with unchanged small glyphs. The source prefers announced wallets over the legacy injected fallback, following [EIP-6963](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-6963), and exposes the official test-ETH faucet as a quiet external link.
