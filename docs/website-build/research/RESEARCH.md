# Website reference research and synthesis

4 November 2026 (Asia/Bangkok). Historical research behind the website direction. The owner selected Morning Gate's colour/atmosphere and Little Life Utility's layout; their selected references remain in [Morning Gate](../../../design/references/website-morning-gate.png) and [Utility](../../../design/references/website-utility.png). The current hybrid build guide is [here](../../../design/website-build/mockups/index.html).

## Scope and evidence

Three researchers examined official cozy/pet/pixel games, onchain/NFT/AI games, and documentation/visitor journeys. Their authored notes are retained alongside this report: [pet games](research-pet-games.md), [onchain games and docs](research-crypto-docs.md), and [content architecture](research-content-architecture.md). The PM opened the sites in the dedicated Codex Browser profile and inspected desktop and selected phone renders. The superseded screenshot/extraction collection was archived during public-repository cleanup; the public research keeps our observations and official source links. Those captures were study references, not proposed assets or endorsement. Browser rendering was a sampled state, not a performance benchmark or full-site usability audit.

## What the actual rendered references taught us

| Official reference | Observed presentation | Useful inference for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Stardew Valley](https://www.stardewvalley.net/) | Full-width pixel landscape, huge pixel wordmark, compact blue navigation. Genre is immediate before explanatory copy. | The house and garden can do much of the introduction. Keep our single app entrance more obvious than this broader franchise navigation. |
| [Sunflower Land](https://sunflower-land.com/) | Bright blue stage, large pixel headline, cutaway farm island, warm Play button and direct Docs link. At 390px the hero copy/metrics/promos push most world art beneath the fold. | Strong game-world + browser-app + docs comparison. Put world, promise and Open App together on phones; omit competing promo, metric and economic strips. Its free/no-wallet copy does not apply to our mint flow. |
| [Pixels](https://www.pixels.xyz/) | Vivid violet field, acid-yellow pixel logo/buttons, crisp world strip. Three equally emphatic hero actions compete. | Vibrancy can coexist with pixel art. Preserve a single main action instead of copying staking/community alongside Play. |
| [Neko Atsume](https://www.nekoatsume.com/en/) and [How to Play](https://www.nekoatsume.com/en/about.html) | Three friendly illustrated navigation choices; instructions pair actual phone screenshots with a small amount of explanatory text and lots of breathing room. | Watching is a valid proposition. Use concrete little moments and three verbs, with docs separate. Its cartoon cats are reference subjects, not an art style for our rooster. |
| [Usagi Shima](https://usagishima.net/) | Large authored environment and wordmark on warm paper; just four secondary routes. Latest release art occupies the first viewport. | A tiny project can feel personal and complete without ecosystem-scale menus. Keep our game explanation above future news/merchandise. |
| [Tamagotchi Paradise](https://tamagotchi-official.com/us/series/paradise/) | Loud character/product art, deep purple and bright pink/cyan, several promotional layers. Captured phone state was sparse/incomplete. | Emotional pet identity is valuable; catalogue and cartoon density are not appropriate to our small game. Do not infer successful mobile adaptation from the desktop design. |
| [Aavegotchi](https://www.aavegotchi.com/) | Immersive pink character world, layered news/network strip and dense crypto navigation, several game/economy actions. | Ownership can be embodied by a specific life. We should simplify the surrounding economy and avoid copying earning claims. |
| [Parallel TCG](https://parallel.life/game) and [Colony](https://parallel.life/colony) | Large cinematic dark stage, emphatic wordmark, restrained borders, high-contrast primary action; text explains actual play. | Allow one large atmospheric environment, but our pixels/cosy scale should avoid sci-fi video weight or a mandatory trailer. AI should be explained through its role in a day. |
| [Provable Games](https://provable.games/) | Large editorial type, sparse dark hero, one Explore button and quieter docs link. Later content separates game, collectibles and community. | Typographic confidence can accompany game art; no need for a generic six-card crypto landing page. Its unstoppable/trust claims cannot be transferred to our hosted relay. |
| [Fren Pet](https://frenpet.xyz/) | Captured desktop had atmospheric side art and a centered Loading state; extractor also saw Loading. | Useful bounded observation of a dependency-heavy entry. Our website and docs should show useful content and Open App without waiting for the game runtime. This sample does not establish a persistent defect or measured latency. |
| [Sunflower docs](https://docs.sunflower-land.com/) | Grouped left navigation, clear article surface, search, next topic; much larger feature catalogue than ours. | Player task groups first; avoid adopting its entire encyclopaedia or leading with an investment disclaimer panel. |
| [Fren Pet docs](https://fren-pet-docs.vercel.app/) | Left topic list, main article, right contents, clear route back to Website. | An expressive homepage can become conventional readable reference. Use a more bounded first-day article instead of listing every system at once. |
| [NEAR docs](https://docs.near.org/) | Search and topic navigation; task starts and larger reading type. Phone removes desktop columns. | Useful search/article/contents patterns; no permanently consuming desktop sidebar on a narrow phone. Its developer taxonomy is too technical for our player guide. |
| [Current Aavegotchi docs](https://docs.aavegotchi.com/) | Search, grouped guide sidebar and a distinct Dapp exit. | Docs should always have a direct app exit. Historical official pages can disagree with current deployments: verify our wallet/world facts against code/config before a future release. |

The broader linked research also covers official Axie, Guild of Guardians, Neopets, WEBFISHING and adjacent Uniswap information design. Those are textual/navigation references, not claimed rendered-screen reviews. Access failures and unofficial lookalike sites were recorded rather than silently replaced with unreliable evidence.

A small navigation follow-up also clicked Neko Atsume's How to Play and Fren Pet's Overview → Gameplay, recording their actual destination pages and captures in handoffs.json. At the sampled 390px width, Neko's how-to retained a wider layout and clipped navigation/article text; its screenshot-and-copy grouping is useful, but its fixed-width phone treatment is a caution, not a pattern to copy. Sunflower's Play link explicitly targets /play; the captured original tab remained on the marketing page, so its after-click image is labelled **original-tab-after-play**, not presented as an app onboarding screenshot. No wallet, signup, purchase or gameplay operation was performed on another project.

## Selected structure to explore, not a selected visual style

**Home → The game → Guide**, with a conspicuous **Open App** throughout. A new visitor learns the emotional premise and sees the world; the app handles wallet/world/hatch at an explicit owner action. Returning owners enter directly. Shared exact-life links keep their existing direct destination. The website is an introduction and reference layer, not a new authentication flow.

Three mockups per direction cover the home, a visual play explanation, and a proper guide. The six designers get the same factual brief and complete research. Broad composition territories keep the options distinct while leaving each designer control over its expression.

Keep claims grounded: testnet worlds only, no bridge, no guaranteed income, no NPC trader, no offline aging, no trustless inference claim. Keep new-day Jev choices sealed. The canonical procedural rooster remains untouched; environmental art in mockups is exploratory and any production portrait uses the existing generator.

## Proposed lightweight behavior after selection

The site should paint readable content immediately; a few small sprite/environment loops can make it alive. Open App never waits for a film. Docs use ordinary readable body typography and quiet article surfaces. Load the game only on entry, preserve existing app/NFT/recovery URLs, and use one shared content source for wallet/world/deployment facts. These are design recommendations, not implemented behavior or a comparative speed claim.

No main push or deployment is part of this exploration. The owner will choose or combine directions before website implementation.
