## Actual NFT portrait art

The old image was a96px diagram with a header, flat room and tiny character. Independent critiques drove successive improvements to the new160px illustration: stronger character focus, two seeded environment orientations, three large roof/entrance profiles, four material finishes, six palettes, coherent shadows/step planes, grouped foreground foliage and no embedded debug-like inscription.

The original rooster remains an exact integer3× canonical group, unmirrored and ungraded. The environment alone varies cosmetically by seed. Plants, growth, items and household conditions derive from saved facts; no new actions, crops, ownership or rewards are fabricated.
