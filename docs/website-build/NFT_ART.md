# The NFT's illustrated home

The actual NFT image is a self-contained SVG generated by `server/v2/portrait.ts`. Both `server/v2/http.ts` (Robinhood) and `server/near/http.ts` (native NEAR) already use this shared renderer. Their metadata image addresses, NFT identity and game state formats are unchanged.

The portrait now uses a 160 × 160 pixel scene, exported at 640 × 640. A close view of the rooster stands on a sunlit stone courtyard beside its cottage and four real garden beds. Ceramic roof tiles, inset window glass and curtains, timber, foundation masonry, a recessed doorway, flagstone grain, landscape depth and block-edged environmental shadows replace the former flat room and status header. Warm color, strong green foliage, a cropped canopy and grouped broad leaves frame the subject. Four seed-based architectural finishes—clapboards, timber and lime plaster, ochre plaster with stone, or limestone courses—give the cottages different material identities. Three roof and entrance profiles change the larger shapes: a deep projecting tiled eave, a pitched entrance hood, or a short stone-cottage roof with broad steps. The seed also chooses a garden on the left or right. The illustration has no nameplate, numerical header or floor inscription; complete identity and day remain in metadata and the accessible SVG title.

## Canonical identity

`src/art/rooster.ts` and `src/art/rooster-data.ts` remain unchanged. The renderer inserts the exact output of `roosterSvg(state.seed, 32, state.alive ? 'idle' : 'dead')`, replacing only its outer SVG wrapper with a group. The group is translated on integer coordinates and scaled by exactly 3. The original shape, palette, headgear and pose pixels are neither redrawn nor filtered. Its visible height is roughly 40% of the image, making it the focal subject at explorer thumbnail sizes. Only the environment is mirrored in the garden-left layout. The rooster is a separate group with a positive scale, placed on its porch.

## What changes and why

The image is a pure, deterministic function of the supplied public name and saved state. It never consults a clock, pending request, plan, future model choice or replay presentation state. Refreshing an unchanged life produces the same SVG.

| Visible detail | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Rooster shape, colors and accessory | Existing canonical generator with the NFT's unchanged seed |
| Two environment orientations, three roof and entrance profiles, six architectural palettes, four wall materials, sky atmosphere, curtain and door details, material texture | A separate cosmetic hash of the same permanent seed |
| Accessible SVG title with full name and day | Public name and saved `state.day` |
| Alive or remembered pose and world treatment | Saved `state.alive`; remembered world is muted behind an unfiltered canonical dead pose |
| Four planted or empty beds | The four saved `state.plots` entries, in their original index order within the seed's permanent layout; orientation does not exchange or change plot state |
| Plant kind | Actual saved `plot.kind`, using all four flowers, four fruits and the wood tree |
| Initial growth, ripe harvest and repeat growth | Saved `ticks` and `left`, interpreted against the existing `PLANTS` catalog |
| Soil and foliage | Saved moisture, dryness and soil blocking |
| Plot covers and garden shelter | Saved `umbrellaLife` entries and `shelterLife` |
| Wood, toolkit, spoon, toy boat, birdhouse and large chest | Their positive quantities in saved inventory; no item appears on the bench merely because it might be crafted |
| Chair and its position | Saved chair life and anchor |
| Broken stove, window or chair; patches, braces, mess, fire and power outage | Their corresponding saved house fields |

The cottage, its permanent roof/entrance shape, fixed workbench/stove/window, distant hills, fence and cropped wild canopy are environmental scenery. They do not assert an extra inventory item, planted crop, earned reward or player-built improvement. A cosmetic doorway hood or eave is unrelated to the actual garden shelter: only positive saved `shelterLife` draws the functional garden cover. The seed-based sky is a permanent artistic atmosphere, not current weather or the simulation's time of day. Newly planted crops start as sprouts; flower blooms and fruit are drawn only when saved `ticks` is zero. Repeat vines and trees keep their grown structure while an actual later yield is regrowing.

The SVG's XML-escaped title preserves the full public name without a text label in the illustration. Metadata remains the source for complete identity and numerical traits.

## Portability and checks

The SVG contains its own rectangle/path artwork and local clipping references. It has no external image, external font, script, stylesheet, filter, animation or `foreignObject`. Both living and remembered art have an opaque background, keeping explorer light/dark page colors from changing the illustration.

The focused checks are run with:

```sh
npm run check
npx tsx --test server/v2/portrait.test.ts
```

Ten renderer tests cover exact canonical pixels across five identities in both life states, deterministic output and immutable input, XML-safe names, standalone dependencies, opaque remembered art, both orientations without mirroring or grading the rooster or reordering saved beds, empty beds, all nine ripe plants without premature harvest, mature repeat structures and real inventory changes that preserve the rooster.

Twelve controlled visual fixtures were rendered as standalone SVGs and PNGs, plus contact sheets at 180 and 80 pixels per portrait. These are illustrative state fixtures, not claims about real onchain actions or Jev decisions. Scratch fixture generators, runtime dependencies and outputs are kept in ignored `TEMP/nft-portrait/`; production code does not depend on them. The standalone SVG fixtures ranged from approximately 47 to 62 KB before transport compression. No wallet, contract, game rule, metadata route, chain identifier, deployment or token operation is changed by the art renderer.
