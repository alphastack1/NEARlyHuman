# Individual routines and a clearer first visit — 1 November 2026

This pass addresses two owner requests: make different NPCs/days less repetitive, and teach the experience through obvious actions rather than a tutorial wall. Parallel owners handled the decision policy, renderer and first-use UI; two independent critics compared actual saved days and approached the public game without its guide. The PM reviewed pixels and operational integration.

The permanent [comparison gallery](variety-review/index.html) includes first-use screens, physical/event comparisons, a motion clip and a timing chart. The build and review artifacts work without `TEMP/`.

## What was actually wrong

The five existing saved days contain **257 quiet receipts out of300**. BOK and PEEP's first days have exactly the same ten meaningful actions, targets, variants and slots. Both spend all bread by slot4 and all water by21;36 of43 meaningful actions across the five days occur before Midday. The old prompt contains generic objectives and no stable individual preferences. Its six-entry memory ends each day with wandering rather than meaningful earlier activities.

Fresh visitors face a wallet gate before seeing the creature act. Creation exposes trait allocation before the player understands a day. Preparation ends at another Ready/Watch decision, while returning owners find yesterday through Saved days. These are ordering and feedback problems, not missing explanatory paragraphs.

## What remains worth improving

The result supports different habits and different days, not a claim that repetition is solved. MISO eats plain bread; NORI makes toast on day1 and has a coffee ritual/milk on day2 while keeping its seed/profile. Supplies, initial needs and accepted RNG seeds also differ, so the live cohort does not isolate personality alone as the cause.

Quiet still occupies160/180 canonical receipts (88.9%). NORI day2 has37 consecutive quiet receipts and leaves its available rose seed unused despite planting being offered. A receipt count is not a film-duration measurement, but it clearly identifies more room to improve the watching experience and supply consequences. Prior-day memory/long-term learning is not implemented by the current exact current-day memory. The existing three-day testnet limit also remains.

The next proposed loops are quiet-day viewing pace, supply-driven household projects, and persistent individual continuity/progression. These are suggestions for the owner, not additional work started without their choice.
